PocketQuery Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026 · 한국어: https://cdhnoel-hash.github.io/pocketquery/privacy/

Choi Donghan (the "operator") cares about the privacy of people who use the Android app PocketQuery (the "app") and complies with the Personal Information Protection Act of Korea and other applicable laws. This policy explains what information the app keeps, where it keeps it, and who it is exchanged with.

1. Summary

  • The operator does not run any servers and does not send or collect app users' information.
  • Connection details, passwords and query history are stored only on your device. Passwords and SSH keys are encrypted with the Android Keystore.
  • Queries and data are exchanged only with the database servers (and SSH servers) you set up yourself.
  • To show ads in the app, Google AdMob collects device information such as the advertising ID (section 4). If you buy "Remove ads", the app does not start the ad SDK.
  • In-app purchases are handled by Google Play. The operator does not receive your payment details.

2. Information stored only on your device

The app stores the following information in its private storage on your device to provide its features. None of it is sent to the operator.

  • When you export results or create backups, files are saved only to the location you pick (file picker) or sent only to the app you choose to share with.
  • App lock uses Android biometric and screen lock authentication (BiometricPrompt). Fingerprint and face data are handled by the Android system; the app has no access to them.

3. Information exchanged with servers you specify

The app connects to the database servers (PostgreSQL, MySQL/MariaDB, SQL Server) and SSH servers you enter, and exchanges account credentials, the SQL you run and its results with them. These servers are managed by you (or your organization), and how they handle information is governed by their administrators' policies. The operator does not receive this traffic.

Connections without TLS may send passwords and data unencrypted, so the app recommends using TLS or an SSH tunnel.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows a Google AdMob banner ad at the bottom of the connection list. To serve ads, Google may collect and process information such as:

  • Device identifiers such as the advertising ID (Android Advertising ID) and app set ID
  • IP address (and approximate location derived from it), device and OS information, app information
  • Ad interactions such as impressions and clicks, and diagnostic information (SDK errors etc.)

This information is used to show ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and, if you consent, show personalized ads. See Google's documentation for details:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Data collected by AdMob (Play data safety disclosure): https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure

Your choices

  • In regions that require consent, such as the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK and Switzerland, the app shows the Google UMP (User Messaging Platform) consent form on first launch. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time in the app's Settings → "Ad privacy settings".
  • If you live in a US state with a privacy law, such as California, you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information for advertising ("Do Not Sell or Share") in the app's Settings → "Ad privacy settings". If you opt out, Google serves ads with restricted data processing.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • If you buy "Remove ads", the app does not show the consent form and does not initialize the ad SDK.

5. In-app purchases (Google Play)

The "Remove ads" purchase is processed by the Google Play billing system. Payment methods and payment information are handled by Google; the operator does not receive them. To check that a purchase is valid, the app checks the purchase information Google Play provides to the device (product ID, purchase state, purchase token) on the device only.

  • Google Play Terms of Service and Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

8. Children

The app is a development and operations tool for working with databases and is not directed at children under 14 (under 13 in the United States).

9. Deleting information and your rights

  • In the app: Edit connection → "Delete this connection", query history "Clear all", delete snippets
  • Delete everything: Android Settings → Apps → PocketQuery → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app. All information on the device is deleted
  • Because the operator does not keep your information, there is no information to request access to, correct or delete from the operator. Information processed by Google can be managed in your Google Account settings (https://myaccount.google.com) and with the options in section 4

10. Security measures

  • Passwords and SSH keys are encrypted with an Android Keystore key and never written to logs
  • App data is excluded from cloud backup and device-to-device transfer
  • TLS certificate verification and SSH server fingerprint checks are provided
  • App lock and screen protection (hiding the app in recent apps and screenshots) are provided
